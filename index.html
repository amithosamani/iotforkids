THE TWO-DAY PLAN

What we’ll teach, hour by hour.

Each day runs 9:00 am to 4:00 pm with tea breaks and a lunch hour. About 70% of the time, students are wiring, testing and changing simple programs in teams of 3 to 4.

On Day 2, every team builds one invention

Smart dustbinLid opens when a hand comes near
Rain alertBuzzer and light warn when it rains
Plant helperTells you when a plant needs water
Fire alarmTeacher-run flame-sensor demo
Mini weather stationShows temperature and humidity
Fan controllerMotor starts when it gets hot

By the end, students can

  • Explain IoT in everyday words
  • Tell sensors (inputs) from actuators (outputs)
  • Wire a part to an Arduino safely, from a diagram
  • Upload a ready program and change a value to change the result
  • Present their invention as Sense → Think → Act
Safety first. Boards are unplugged before any wire is changed, only the teacher runs the flame demo, and water and batteries are handled under supervision.
HOW IT WORKS

What we do, and how we do it.

  1. 1

    Learn the big idea

    A short story and a live demo show how smart things Sense, Think and Act.

  2. 2

    Meet the parts

    Kids touch and test 17 real components: sensors, lights, a buzzer, a motor and more.

  3. 3

    Wire it and run it

    They follow a simple diagram, plug in a ready program and change one value to see the result change.

  4. 4

    Build and present

    In teams they build one of six inventions and explain it as Sense → Think → Act.

A little about the person behind it

Hi, I'm Amit.

I'm a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student, and I enjoy learning how electronics, sensors, and connected devices work.

I started thinking about IoT for Kids because technology can feel complicated when it's only explained in a textbook. I want younger students to get a chance to see it in action, ask questions, and try things for themselves.

This is a learning initiative I'm building step by step, with school needs and student safety in mind.

The aim isn't to make every child an engineer. It's to help them stay curious.

For parents and guardians

Your child brought this home. Here's the idea.

IoT for Kids is a proposed two-day workshop that helps students explore how smart devices work. Children will learn about sensors, see demonstrations, and take part in beginner-friendly activities suited to their grade.

Dates, fees, equipment, and participation arrangements will be confirmed with the school. If you'd like to ask a question, you can use the contact section below.

Does my child need coding experience?

No previous coding experience is intended to be necessary. The workshop begins with simple examples and guided activities.

What will students actually do?

They'll explore IoT examples, learn what sensors do, watch demonstrations, and try a practical activity if equipment and school arrangements allow.

When is the workshop and how much does it cost?

Dates and fees are not confirmed yet. These details will be shared after discussion with the school.

How will safety be handled?

Activities should be age-appropriate and supervised. Equipment and safety arrangements will be discussed with the school in advance.
